General Overview

Papakura is a local board area located in southern Auckland, New Zealand. It extends from Drury in the south to Alfriston in the north, and includes Takanini, Hingaia, Red Hill, and Pahurehure. Papakura is governed under the jurisdiction of the Auckland Council and forms part of the Manurewa-Papakura Ward.
